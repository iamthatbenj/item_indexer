# Retain high-quality photos rather than camera originals

The initial service targets an operating budget below $10/month. Retain high-quality optimized photos rather than promising permanent storage of camera originals, accepting irreversible loss of some resolution in exchange for a smaller storage footprint. Start with a maximum long edge of 3,200 pixels, never upscale smaller images, and validate compression against representative item photos, maker's marks, and receipt text rather than forcing a tiny file-size limit.

Strip hidden photo metadata, including GPS, device details, and embedded timestamps, while preserving correct visual orientation and colour. Meaningful historical dates belong in explicit contributions, not inferred camera metadata.

Portable archives contain the retained photos, not necessarily camera originals. Make this clear before upload. This supersedes the earlier discussion's original-photo preservation promise; it does not establish how non-image documents are processed. Vercel Blob remains provisional pending cost verification.
