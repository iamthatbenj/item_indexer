# Family Belongings

A family record of personal belongings and the history and meaning attached to them.

## Language

**Family collection**:
A private record of belongings shared by a defined group of family members, independent of where those belongings are kept. Each family collection has its own membership; a person may participate in more than one.
_Avoid_: Household, location

**Accession note**:
A record of an item's entry into a family collection, including the circumstances of its arrival.

**De-accession note**:
A record of an item's departure from a family collection, including the circumstances of its departure. Departure does not erase the item's history from that collection.

**Item**:
A personal belonging or set of belongings documented as one record by the family, together with its history and sentimental significance. The family chooses whether a set is recorded together or as separate items.
_Avoid_: Asset, product, listing

**Category**:
A shared label grouping similar items within a family collection, such as furniture or jewellery.
_Avoid_: Folder

**Location**:
A shared label describing where an item is currently kept, independent of collection membership or permission to view it.
_Avoid_: Family collection, household

**Current keeper**:
The person currently caring for an item, whether or not they participate in the family collection. Keeping an item does not establish legal ownership.
_Avoid_: Owner

**Draft item**:
An unfinished item record captured with at least one photo, whose identifying name or description may still be missing.
_Avoid_: Complete entry

**Person**:
A named individual referenced within a family collection, optionally distinguished by a brief identifying note. A person may be a storyteller or current keeper without being an app participant.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Storyteller**:
The person whose recollection a story expresses, distinct from whoever records or edits it.
_Avoid_: Author, editor

**Family member**:
A person invited to participate in the family's shared record, with the same ability to edit item records as other participating relatives.
_Avoid_: Curator, viewer

**Family administrator**:
A family member entrusted with managing invitations and access to the family's private record. This responsibility does not give their recollections or item edits greater authority than those of other family members.
_Avoid_: Curator, owner

**Supporting evidence**:
An attached image or document supporting an item's record, classified as a purchase receipt, comparable listing or result, or other document. It may include source details, dates, amounts, and explanatory notes.

**Purchase receipt**:
Evidence of a purchase of the recorded item itself, rather than a transaction involving a similar item. A historical purchase price is not necessarily its current value.

**Comparable**:
Evidence about a similar item offered for sale or sold elsewhere, used to inform a possible financial estimate for an item. An asking price is not a confirmed sale price, and a comparable is not itself a valuation.
_Avoid_: Valuation, appraisal

**Story**:
An attributed recollection about an item, credited to the person whose memory it expresses, who may differ from the family member recording it. Different stories about the same item may disagree without either being discarded.
_Avoid_: Verified history, canonical account
